About SoundBridge

Mission Statement: To empower professional music educators with intuitive, high-performance digital tools that streamline studio operations, enhance student practice engagement, and elevate the standard of online music education worldwide.

Founded by Helen Lee (Owner, CEO, and Product Architect), SoundBridge bridges the critical gap between live instruction and asynchronous practice reinforcement. Operating as a Domestic LLC in the United States, our vision extends from individual studio optimization to a future global student-matching marketplace.

Initial Market

U.S. independent teachers & commercial academies (Ages 16–60).
